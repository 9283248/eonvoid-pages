FAQ

Where do the numbers come from? Monthly historical closes (2010–2026) bundled inside the app — it works fully offline.

Is this financial advice? No. MoneyRewind is a simulator for education and fun. Past performance does not predict future results.

What does Pro unlock? All 13 assets, monthly-investing (DCA) mode, FORWARD projections on market profiles, and clean share cards.

How do I cancel my subscription? iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → MoneyRewind Pro.

Restore on a new device? MENU → RESTORE PURCHASES (same Apple ID).
