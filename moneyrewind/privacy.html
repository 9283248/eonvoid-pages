MONEYREWIND

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: July 17, 2026

1. The Short Version

MoneyRewind ("the App") is an offline investment time machine: it simulates what past investments would be worth using historical market data bundled inside the app. Everything runs on your device. No server, no account, no analytics, no personal data collected.

2. What We Collect

Nothing. The App has no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Your scenarios never leave your device.

3. Photo Library

If you save a result card, the App asks for add-only access to your photo library. It cannot read your photos.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

Preferences (chosen asset, onboarding state) are stored locally and are removed when you delete the App.

5. In-App Purchases

MoneyRewind Pro is an auto-renewing subscription processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
