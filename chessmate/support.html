FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the unlock screen from a locked world and tap “Restore Purchases”, or use “Restore Purchases” in your profile.
Is it a subscription?
No. “Unlock All Worlds” is a one-time purchase.
Does it work offline?
Yes, completely. Puzzles are generated on your iPhone.
I'm stuck on a puzzle.
Tap “Hint” to highlight the right piece. Boss levels (every 5th level) have no hints — that's the challenge.
How do I turn off sounds?
Tap your rank piece or XP on the home screen and switch “Sound effects” off. Sounds also follow the silent switch.
Where is my progress stored?
Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app resets it.
