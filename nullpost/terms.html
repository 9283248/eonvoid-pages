What Nullpost is

Nullpost sells collectible engraved stamps as In-App Purchases and calculates which of them add up to an amount you specify. People commonly use it to spend a leftover Apple Account balance, because Apple requires a zero balance before you can change your store region.

We are not Apple. We cannot change your region, read your balance, or refund a purchase — all of that stays between you and Apple.

What you get

Each purchase gives you a stamp: its artwork, its issue year, a place in your album, and the right to print it on a sheet. Stamps are consumable, so the same stamp can be bought again later.

What we do not promise

  • We do not promise that Apple will let you change region — that depends on your subscriptions, payment method and family sharing settings, which we explain but do not control.
  • Prices come from Apple and differ by country. Some balances cannot be cleared exactly, which the app tells you honestly before you buy anything.
  • Taxes in some regions are added on top of the price. Where that happens, an exact zero may not be reachable by purchases alone.

Refunds

All purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. A refund returns the money to the same Apple Account balance you were clearing.
