Support

Where do I find my balance?

Open the App Store, tap your profile photo at the top right — the amount is shown under your name. Nullpost cannot read it: Apple gives no app that access, which is why you type it in.

Why does it take several confirmations?

App Store has no batch payment: every purchase is confirmed separately. That is exactly why Nullpost works out the plan with the fewest possible purchases instead of dumping a pile of small ones on you.

A few cents are left and nothing fits

Apple’s prices only ever end in certain cents, so some small remainders cannot be assembled at all. The app shows you the shortest way out: top the balance up by a small amount that makes it divisible again, then clear it in one or two purchases. If topping up is not an option, ask Apple Support to zero the remainder.

I paid and the balance did not reach zero

Check whether tax was added on top of the price — in the United States and Canada it usually is. Re-enter the new balance and the app will recalculate the plan.

Can I get my money back?

Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Remember that a refund lands back on the balance you were clearing.

Still stuck

Email 9283248@gmail.com — include your country and the exact balance, that is usually enough to reproduce it.
