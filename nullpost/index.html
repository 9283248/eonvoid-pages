Clear your balance

Stuck at “your balance must be zero”?

Apple will not let you change your App Store country while any money is left on the account. Nullpost works out which purchases add up to your remainder exactly, in as few confirmations as possible — and every purchase leaves you a collectible engraved stamp for your album, printable on A4 or US Letter.

Typical remainder: three confirmations instead of twenty-nine.

Honest before you pay

The app shows what can and cannot be cleared in your currency before the first purchase, and tells you when topping up is cheaper than grinding through small stamps.
