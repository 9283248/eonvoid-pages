Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

The short version

Nullpost collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs. Everything the app does happens on your device.

The app cannot read your Apple Account balance. Apple gives no app that access — you type the amount in yourself, and it never leaves your iPhone.

What we do not collect

  • No personal information, sign-ups or accounts
  • No Apple Account credentials — we never ask for your password and could not use it if you gave it to us
  • No analytics, crash trackers, advertising or attribution SDKs
  • No location, contacts, photos or usage data

The balance you enter

The amount you type is used only to calculate which stamps add up to it. It is held in memory while the app is open and is never uploaded, stored on a server or shared.

Your stamp album

Stamps you buy are recorded on your device so the album survives a restart. That record stays on the iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Stamps are In-App Purchases processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Refunds are handled by Apple — and a refunded purchase returns to the very balance you were clearing. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Printing

The printable sheet is generated on your device. If you save it, it goes to your own Photos or Files — nowhere else.

Children

Nullpost is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
