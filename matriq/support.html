Frequently asked

How is the score calculated?

Matriq uses an item-response model (Rasch, corrected for guessing). Each task carries an estimated difficulty; your answers move an ability estimate up or down, and the test adapts by picking the next task near your current level. The estimate is then mapped onto a 100 ± 15 scale and shown together with a confidence range, because a 20-task test cannot be more precise than roughly ±13 points. Speed does not affect the score. The model is calibrated on this app's own task bank.

Why do the tasks never repeat?

There is no question bank inside Matriq. Every matrix, analogy, sequence, odd-one-out and number series is drawn by an algorithm at the moment you see it, from a random seed. That is why the test cannot be memorized or looked up — and why a retest is a genuine new test rather than the same questions again.

What is free and what does Pro unlock?

Free: the full 20-task test, your score, the confidence range and your facet profile, an explanation for the first 3 tasks, and the first two chapters of the scale. Matriq Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks a step-by-step explanation for every task, the remaining chapters, unlimited practice and attempt history. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

On a new device, or after reinstalling: open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase. The purchase is permanent and does not expire.

A task looks wrong or has two possible answers. What do I do?

Use Settings → Report this question (also available from the explanation screen). It opens your mail app with the task type and its seed already filled in — that seed lets us reproduce the exact task and fix the generator. Sending the message as-is is enough.

Does the app work offline, and where is my data?

Yes, entirely — tasks are generated on the device. Attempts, progress and settings are stored only on your iPhone and never leave it; the app has no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

Matriq gives an IQ-style estimate for practice and entertainment. It is not a clinical assessment.
