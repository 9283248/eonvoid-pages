MATRIQ

Privacy policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

1. The short version

Matriq does not collect any data. None. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting and no server of our own. The app does not send your answers, scores or usage anywhere, because it has nowhere to send them.

2. What stays on your device

Everything the app remembers lives only in the app's own storage on your iPhone:

  • your test attempts, scores and facet profile;
  • progress on the scale — chapters, nodes, streak;
  • settings such as theme, haptics and daily goal;
  • whether Matriq Pro has been unlocked.

We cannot see any of it. Deleting the app removes all of it from your device.

3. Network use

Matriq works fully offline — every task is generated on your device. The only moment the network is involved is a purchase or a restore of a purchase, which is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never receive or store your payment details.

4. In-app purchase

Matriq Pro is a single one-time purchase (not a subscription), processed by Apple. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase exists for your Apple ID. No personal information reaches us.

5. Support email

If you write to us — or use "Report this question", which opens your mail app with the task details prefilled — we see the address you write from and whatever you put in the message. We use it only to answer you, and we do not add it to any list.

6. Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: 9283248@gmail.com

Matriq gives an IQ-style estimate for practice and entertainment. It is not a clinical assessment.
