Common questions

The cards are too small for me. Open Settings (the gear, top right) and turn on Large indices. It enlarges the rank and suit on every card.

I am left-handed. Settings → Left-handed layout moves the deck and waste pile to the right side, under your thumb.

How do I move a card without dragging? Tap it. If there is exactly one sensible place for it, it flies there by itself.

I want three-card draw. Settings → Draw three cards. The change applies to the next deal.

I bought Pro and changed phone. Settings → Restore purchases. It asks Apple for your purchase history under the same Apple ID.

Are there adverts? No. Not banners, not videos, not "watch this to continue". Ever.

Is the deal always winnable? No — deals are shuffled honestly, exactly like a real deck. Undo is unlimited and free, and you can restart the same deal from the New menu.

Baize works with no internet connection at all. If something looks wrong, closing and reopening the app never loses your record.
