Privacy
Last updated: 22 July 2026

The short version

Baize collects nothing. The game runs entirely on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no server of ours for your data to travel to.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings: draw one or three, large indices, left-handed layout, sound, haptics, chosen table.
  • Your record: games played and won, streaks, best time, fewest moves.
  • Whether you unlocked Baize Pro.

All of it lives in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. We never see any of it.

Purchases

Baize Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email, or payment details — Apple simply tells the app whether the purchase exists. Restoring a purchase asks Apple, not us.

Children

Baize is a card game suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new date.

No accounts. No tracking. No adverts. No network requests. A deck of cards and a green table.
