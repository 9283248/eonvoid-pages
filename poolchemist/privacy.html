Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

The short version

Pool Chemist collects no data. The app works entirely on your device.

What we do NOT collect

  • No personal information, accounts or sign-ups
  • Your pool profile, test readings and log never leave your device — they are stored locally
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs
  • No location, contacts or usage data

Offline by design

All chemistry math happens on your iPhone. Pool Chemist makes no network requests.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is a one-time In-App Purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles purchase data.
