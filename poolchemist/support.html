FAQ

Where do the dose numbers come from?

From standard residential pool-care arithmetic (per-10,000-gallon dosing rates used by common charts), scaled to the pool volume you set on the "My Pool" tab. Always follow the chemical product's label.

My doses look too big / too small

Check the pool volume on the My Pool tab — it's the single biggest factor. The volume wizard computes it from your pool's shape and depth, or enter the exact volume if you know it.

What do the target ranges mean?

Defaults follow widely used residential guidance: FC 3–7 ppm, pH 7.4–7.6, TA 60–120 ppm, CYA 30–60 ppm, CH 250–450 ppm. Pro users can customise every range.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

My Pool tab → "Restore Purchases" (also available on the paywall). Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you bought Pro with.

Is my data synced anywhere?

No. Everything is stored locally on your device — the app is fully offline.
