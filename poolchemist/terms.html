What Pool Chemist is

Pool Chemist is an offline calculator that turns water test readings into dosing suggestions for residential pools and hot tubs, with a local log of your tests.

Important safety disclaimer

  • Dosing results are informational estimates based on widely used residential pool-care arithmetic. They are not professional, medical or safety advice.
  • Always read and follow the label of every chemical product — the label overrides anything the app says.
  • Handle pool chemicals with care: add acid to water (never water to acid), never mix chlorine products with acid, store chemicals separately.
