Privacy
Last updated 12 August 2026

The short version

Steady collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK of any kind. The app makes no network requests of its own — it works exactly the same in airplane mode.

The microphone

Steady asks for microphone access for one reason: to work out the pitch of the note you are playing. Audio is analysed in real time on your device, frame by frame, and discarded immediately.

  • Nothing is recorded.
  • Nothing is written to storage.
  • Nothing is transmitted anywhere — there is no server to transmit it to.
  • The microphone is released as soon as you leave the tuner screen or send the app to the background.

If you decline microphone access the metronome still works in full; only the tuner needs to hear your instrument.

What is stored on your device

Only your own settings: the chosen instrument and tuning, reference pitch, tempo, and whether haptics are on. These live in the app's own storage on your iPhone, are never sent anywhere, and are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Steady Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. The App Store tells the app whether the unlock is active — that is all it tells us. We never see your name, your Apple Account, your payment details or your purchase history, and there is no way for us to.

Children

Steady is rated 4+ and is safe for any age. It gathers no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, changes are unlikely to be interesting.
