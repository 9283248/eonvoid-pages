Getting started

Open Steady and play a note — the tuner is already listening. Pick your instrument at the bottom of the screen, or stay in Chromatic mode and it will name whatever it hears.

Why does it say “an octave below” instead of pointing up?

Because that is the moment other tuners break strings. When a lower string is ringing sympathetically, or the detector latches onto a low harmonic, a tuner that simply draws an arrow sends you tightening a peg you should not be touching. Steady checks the octave first and, when the note you are playing is a whole octave under the target, tells you so in words instead. Check you are on the string you think you are on.

The reading looks unstable

  • Play a single string and let it ring — chords have no single pitch to report.
  • Move away from fans, air conditioning and other constant noise.
  • Hold the phone within about half a metre of the instrument.
  • On a very worn or very dead string the pitch genuinely wanders; that is the string, not the app.

The metronome drifts / does not start

Steady schedules its click by counting audio frames, so it does not drift. If nothing is audible, check the silent switch on the side of the phone and make sure Silent mode (Pro) is off — that mode mutes the click deliberately and leaves only the haptic beat.

Does it need the internet?

No. Steady makes no network requests at all. It works in airplane mode, on a plane, in a basement, forever.
