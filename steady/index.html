A tuner that won't tell you to snap a string

Most tuners mistake a low harmonic for the note and confidently point up. Players follow that arrow until something breaks. Steady settles the octave before it says anything, and when you are an octave under the target it says so in words instead of drawing an arrow.

What it does

  • Chromatic tuner that opens already listening — no loading screen, no account, no network.
  • Guitar, bass, ukulele and violin, with the note, octave and exact cents always visible.
  • A strobe display that freezes when the note is true.
  • A metronome that counts audio frames, not timer ticks, so it does not drift.
  • A haptic beat you can practise to in silence.

One payment

The tuner and the metronome are free, with no ads. Steady Pro is a single purchase that unlocks the strobe, alternate tunings, custom reference pitch, silent practice, subdivisions, odd meters and the tempo trainer. There is no subscription.
