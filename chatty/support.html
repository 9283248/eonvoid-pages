Frequently Asked

How do subscriptions work?
Premium unlocks unlimited generations and all prompt templates. Subscriptions auto-renew and can be managed or canceled anytime in your App Store account settings.

How do I restore a purchase?
Open the paywall and tap "Restore Purchases".

Where is my chat history stored?
Locally on your device. You can delete any conversation from the history screen.
