How do I play with more than two people?

Open New game and pick 3 or 4 players. This is part of Ludorace Pro, a one-time purchase.

I bought Pro on another device

Open Settings › Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the purchase screen, while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Can I use real dice?

Yes. In New game choose “My own dice”, roll your dice on the table and tap the number you got.

Is the dice fair?

Yes. Every roll comes from the phone's random number generator and is never adjusted. Tap the chart button during a game to see how often each face came up.

How do I change the language?

Settings › Language: English, Русский, العربية, हिन्दी.
