Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30

Short version: Ludorace does not collect personal data. No account, no ads, no tracking, and the game itself never needs the internet.

What stays on your device

Your unfinished game, the win counts per player, player names you type, and your settings (language, sound, haptics, table mode) are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Dice

Every roll comes from your phone's own random number generator. Rolls are counted on the device to show the “Fair dice” statistics and are not sent anywhere.

Anonymous screen counters

To learn whether the first-run screens and the purchase screen are clear, the app may send anonymous counters to our server: which of these screens was shown and whether a button on it was tapped (for example “purchase screen shown”, “Continue tapped”). Each event is sent at most once per installation. The counters contain no name, no Apple ID, no device identifier, no advertising identifier and no install ID, and they are not linked to you or used for tracking or advertising. As with any internet request, our server sees the IP address of the connection; it is not stored with the counters.

Purchases

The one-time purchase “Ludorace Pro” is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, Apple ID or name. The purchase carries a label that says which screen it was bought from; the label is the same for every buyer of that screen and does not identify you. The app only checks with Apple whether the purchase is active.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
