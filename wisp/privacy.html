Privacy
Last updated: 23 July 2026

The short version

Wisp collects nothing. It runs entirely on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no server of ours for your data to travel to.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings: chosen pattern, wisp colour, session length, haptics and tone toggles.
  • Your history: minutes breathed, session count and day streak.
  • Whether you unlocked Wisp Pro.

All of it lives in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. We never see any of it.

Purchases

Wisp Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email, or payment details — Apple simply tells the app whether the purchase exists. Restoring a purchase asks Apple, not us.

Not medical advice

Wisp is a breathing timer for relaxation. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you have a health concern, please speak with a qualified professional.

Children

Wisp is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new date.

No accounts. No tracking. No adverts. No network requests. Just a light to breathe with.
