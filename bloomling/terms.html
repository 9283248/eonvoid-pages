What is free

Growing a creature is free and complete: one habit, all eight growth stages, the bloom, the shelf and the reminder. Nothing in that cycle is held back.

Bloomling Plus

Plus is an auto-renewing subscription that unlocks the other creature species, the themes and the full set of widgets. It is offered yearly and weekly; the current price is always shown in the app before you confirm, in your own currency.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • If a free trial is offered, any unused part of it is forfeited when you buy a subscription.
