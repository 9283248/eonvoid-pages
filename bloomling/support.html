My creature shrank

That happens after a missed day, and it is meant to. It never costs more than two steps no matter how long you were away, and it never dies. Mark today and it starts growing again.

Can I track more than one habit?

Deliberately not. The whole idea is one habit at a time — a list of twelve is how people fail all twelve. When a creature blooms, its slot frees up and you can start the next one on a different habit.

The widget is empty

Remove the widget and add it again; iOS occasionally holds on to an old snapshot. If it stays empty, write to us — that one is our bug, not yours.

Restoring a subscription

Settings inside the app › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it on.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Everything it knows lives on your device and goes with it — see the privacy policy.
