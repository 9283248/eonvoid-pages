Privacy
Last updated: 24 August 2026

The short version

Bloomling collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests of its own — the only network traffic it can cause is Apple's own, when you buy or restore a subscription.

What stays on your device

  • The habit you typed in, as plain text.
  • Your creature: its species, its growth so far, the day you last marked.
  • Creatures that have bloomed and moved to the shelf.
  • Your reminder time, and whether reminders are on.

All of it is written to a container shared between the app and its widget on the same device. It is never uploaded, because there is nowhere for it to be uploaded to. Deleting the app deletes it.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, Bloomling schedules one local notification a day at the time you chose. It is scheduled by iOS on the device itself: no server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. The app stores one thing about your purchase — whether a subscription is currently active — and stores it on your device so it still works offline.

Children

Bloomling is rated 4+ and is safe for children. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions go to 9283248@gmail.com and are answered by a person.

No tracking. No profiles. No data leaves your phone.
