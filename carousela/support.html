FAQ

How does the AI work? Type a topic and Carousela drafts a full carousel — hook, slides, and CTA — that you can edit. Only your topic text is sent to generate it; your photos never leave your device.

Is my first carousel free? Yes. Your first AI generation is free; unlimited AI and all templates come with Carousela Pro.

What does Pro unlock? Unlimited AI carousels, all 8 templates, all motion presets, and every export format.

Which formats can I export? 4:5 and 1:1 carousels, and 9:16 stories/reels.

How do I cancel? iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Carousela Pro.

The AI didn't respond. Check your connection and try again — generation needs internet. Everything else works offline.
