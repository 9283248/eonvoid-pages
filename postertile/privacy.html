Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

The short version

PosterTile collects no data. None. The app works entirely on your device.

What we do NOT collect

  • No personal information, accounts or sign-ups
  • No photos ever leave your device — tiling and PDF generation happen locally
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs
  • No location, contacts or usage data

Your photos

PosterTile uses Apple's system photo picker, which lets you hand a single image to the app without granting access to your photo library. The selected image is processed on your device only and is never uploaded anywhere.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is a one-time In-App Purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles purchase data.
