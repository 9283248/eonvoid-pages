Layerly

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: July 19, 2026

1. The short version

Layerly runs entirely on your device. It has no account, no server, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Your photos never leave your iPhone through the app.

2. Photos

When you pick a photo, Layerly processes it on-device to compute the layers. The source image and your projects are stored locally on your device and are removed when you delete the app. Nothing is uploaded.

3. What we don't collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or usage analytics. There is no AI service and no network request involved in creating your art — the app works fully offline.

4. In-app purchase

Layerly Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
