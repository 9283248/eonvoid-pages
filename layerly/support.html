FAQ

Does it need internet? No. Everything runs on your device — it works in airplane mode.

Which machines do the files work with? The print-and-cut PDF works with any printer + craft cutter (Cricut/Silhouette Print & Cut). Per-layer PNGs import into most cutting software.

What does Pro unlock? Cut-file exports, print-and-cut PDF, per-layer sheets, up to 5 layers, and watermark-free output — a one-time purchase.

How many layers? 2 to 5. More layers = more depth and more pieces to cut.
