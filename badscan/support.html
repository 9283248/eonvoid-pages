FAQ

Where do my photos go? Nowhere. All presets run on your device; the App has no server and collects no data.

How do I remove the watermark? The watermark strip on free exports is removed by BADSCAN Pro (MENU → UPGRADE, or the paywall).

How do I cancel my subscription? iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → BADSCAN Pro → Cancel. Cancelling keeps Pro until the period ends.

Restore purchases on a new device? Open the App → MENU → RESTORE PURCHASES (same Apple ID).

Video export? Import a video from your library (or the VIDEO button), pick a preset, EXPORT. Video export is a Pro feature.

The camera preview says DEMO. Camera access is off — enable it in iOS Settings → BADSCAN → Camera, or keep printing from your library.
