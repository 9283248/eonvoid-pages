ART SCANNER

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: May 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Art Scanner ("the App") helps artists capture their artwork in high quality and showcase it in interiors. Your artwork is your most valuable asset, and protecting it is central to how the App is built. This Policy explains what we process and why.

2. What We Process

Camera and photos. The App uses your camera to photograph artwork, and — only if you choose to import — your photo library. Capture and basic on-device steps (edge detection, cropping, geometry) happen locally on your device.

Artwork images for enhancement. To produce the enhanced "master" of a scan (glare reduction, color calibration, stitching), an image may be processed on our secure servers. Your full-resolution originals stay on your device; only the data needed for the current job is uploaded, and it is deleted from our processing servers after the job completes.

AI interior generation (optional, Pro). When you ask the App to generate an AI interior around your artwork, the selected image is sent to a third-party AI image-processing provider solely to create the image you requested. Your artwork is not used to train AI models and is not retained by the provider beyond what is needed to return the result.

Account (optional). If you sign in (e.g. Sign in with Apple) to back up or sync your library, we store only minimal account identifiers needed for that purpose.

We do not sell your data or your artwork, and we do not use your artwork to train any models.

3. Shareable Preview Links

If you create a shareable "view in a room" link to send a piece to a collector, the linked preview image is hosted so anyone with the link can view it. You control whether to create such a link, and you can revoke or delete it at any time.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

Your artworks, enhanced masters, metadata (such as titles, sizes, materials, price, sale status), and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Original high-resolution files remain on the device unless you explicitly enable cloud backup.

5. Third-Party Services

The App relies on a limited set of providers: secure cloud processing for image enhancement, a third-party AI provider for optional interior generation, and Apple for in-app purchases. The App does not include third-party advertising networks or cross-app tracking.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Images sent for server processing are deleted after the job completes. You may request deletion of any data associated with you, and you can delete your account from within the App. Contact us at the address below for any data request.

7. In-App Purchases

The App offers an auto-renewing subscription (Pro) through Apple's App Store. Payments are handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive your payment details.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adult artists and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us
