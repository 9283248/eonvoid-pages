SCAN ART · SHOW IT ON THE WALL · SELL IT

How to Use

  • Scan your artwork: Point the camera at your painting — the App detects the edges and guides light, glare, and color for a clean, sale-ready capture
  • See it on a wall: Drop your piece into a ready-made room scene, or generate a custom AI interior (Pro)
  • Frame it: Try frames and mats, and check the outer size before you print
  • Export to sell: Print-ready files for prints/POD, web images for Etsy and your site, and social formats for Instagram & Reels
  • Build a portfolio: Keep your works organized with titles, sizes, materials, and sale status

Tips for the Best Scan

  • Shoot in soft, even light — avoid direct flash and harsh hotspots
  • Hold the phone straight on and parallel to the artwork
  • For glossy oils, use Studio mode and follow the on-screen prompt to tilt away glare
  • Place a color reference card in frame for the most accurate color

FAQ

Q: Do I need an internet connection?
A: Basic capture, cropping, and ready scenes work on device. The enhanced "master" (glare removal, color calibration) and AI interiors are processed in the cloud, so those steps need a connection. If you're offline, you'll get the instant on-device result first and the master finishes later.

Q: Is my artwork safe?
A: Yes. Your original files stay on your device, images sent for processing are deleted after the job, and your artwork is never used to train AI models. See our Privacy Policy.

Q: What can I export?
A: High-resolution images and print-ready files (JPEG/PNG/TIFF/PDF), plus social formats (1:1, 4:5, 9:16). Hi-res without watermark is part of Pro.

Q: What does Pro include?
A: AI interior generation, hi-res export without a watermark, and the full Pro toolkit. It's an auto-renewing subscription.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device. Your plan stays active until the end of the current period.
